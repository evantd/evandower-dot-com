You don't need to overhaul your entire relationship overnight. The principles work through small, consistent investments of time and attention. Gottman's research suggests that just five extra hours a week can dramatically improve your marriage. And the most important thing: keep your standards high.

## The Magic Five Hours

A weekly investment of about five hours that can transform your marriage:

**Partings (5 × 2 min = 10 min/week)**
Before you part each morning, learn about one thing your partner has planned for the day. Two minutes a day, five days a week.

**Reunions (5 × 20 min = 1 hr 40 min/week)**
Engage in a stress-reducing conversation at the end of each workday (see Chapter 5).

**Admiration and Appreciation (5 × 5 min = 25 min/week)**
Find at least one thing every day to genuinely appreciate about your partner, and tell them.

**Affection (5 min/day = 35 min/week)**
Kiss, hold, grab, touch. Make physical contact. The kiss goodbye and hello should last at least six seconds — long enough to feel romantic. Kiss before going to sleep. Let go of grudges before you turn in.

**Weekly Date (2 hours/week)**
A relaxed, low-pressure time together. Ask each other open-ended questions. Turn toward each other. Use the Love Map questions from Chapter 3 for inspiration.

**Total: about 5 hours per week** — a small investment for a big payoff.

## The Marriage "Poop Detector"

Gottman found that happy couples actually have *higher* standards for how they are treated — not lower. They expect kindness, love, and respect as the norm. When something is "off" (hurt feelings, unresolved tension, distance), they address it promptly rather than letting it fester.

Don't lower your expectations. Instead, be vigilant in a constructive way:

- Notice if you're drifting apart
- Notice if one of the Four Horsemen is creeping back
- Use the skills you've learned to address issues quickly
- Don't ignore problems hoping they'll go away

## Putting It All Together

The seven principles build on each other like a house:

1. **Love Maps** (foundation) — know your partner
2. **Fondness and Admiration** — express appreciation
3. **Turn Toward** — connect in small moments
4. **Positive Perspective** — when 1–3 are strong, you naturally give your partner the benefit of the doubt
5. **Manage Conflict** — solve solvable problems, dialogue about perpetual ones
6. **Make Dreams Come True** — honor each other's aspirations
7. **Shared Meaning** (top) — create your own culture

The two walls holding up the entire house: **Trust** and **Commitment**.

Don't worry about missteps. If you stumble, come back to the book and the exercises. The fact that you're working on your marriage at all puts you ahead.
